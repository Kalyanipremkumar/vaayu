# Vaayu — Art Valuation Factors (reference)

The complete set of factors a credible art valuation weighs. Use this to (a) decide
which inputs the app should collect, and (b) sharpen the pricing prompt. Today the app
uses only a subset (tradition, medium, dimensions, condition, artist tier, provenance
notes) — the rest are the roadmap for accuracy.

## 1. The work itself (intrinsic)

- **Medium & materials** — oil, acrylic, natural/mineral pigment, ink, gouache, mixed
  media; substrate (handmade paper, cloth, canvas, board, silk); special materials
  (gold/silver leaf, semi-precious pigments) carry premiums.
- **Technique & craftsmanship** — fineness and difficulty of execution. Tradition-specific
  styles materially change value: Madhubani _Kachni_ (fine line) vs _Bharni_ (colour fill)
  vs _Godna_ vs _Tantrik_; Warli line density; Pattachitra detailing.
- **Size / dimensions** — value per sq ft is **non-linear**; very large statement pieces and
  very small works can both command premiums; awkward sizes discount.
- **Condition & conservation** — tears, foxing, fading, flaking, water/insect damage,
  prior restoration (sympathetic vs heavy-handed), structural integrity.
- **Subject / iconography / theme rarity** — desirable or rare themes (e.g. Nari-Kunjar,
  specific deities, narrative scenes) lift value; generic/decorative subjects sit lower.
- **Composition & aesthetic quality** — balance, originality, "wall power".
- **Palette & pigments** — natural vs synthetic; characteristic vs off-tradition colour.
- **Date / period / age** — early-career vs mature-period; historically significant periods.
- **Uniqueness vs edition** — unique original vs print/serigraph; edition size; artist's
  proofs; reproductions.
- **Signature / inscription / dating on the work** — presence, placement, legibility.
- **Framing / mounting / presentation** — archival mounting adds; poor framing detracts.

## 2. The artist (attribution & reputation)

- **Attribution certainty** — signed & documented > attributed > "school of" > "after".
- **Recognition tier** — emerging → mid-career → established → blue-chip (drives Layer 2).
- **Master / honours status** — GI-tag association, National/State awards, Padma honours,
  Shilp Guru, master-artisan lineage.
- **Exhibition history** — solo vs group; prestige of venues/institutions/biennales.
- **Auction record** — hammer prices, sell-through rate, price trajectory over time.
- **Gallery representation & primary-market prices** — who represents them, list prices.
- **Critical reception** — press, monographs, catalogues, scholarship.
- **Living vs deceased** — estate dynamics, fixed vs growing supply.
- **Career stage & output volume** — scarcity vs market saturation.
- **Collector base & demand depth** — how many active buyers want this artist.

## 3. Provenance & documentation

- **Ownership chain** — unbroken provenance adds confidence and value.
- **Certificate of authenticity** — from artist, estate, gallery, or recognised expert.
- **Purchase invoices / receipts** — gallery or auction paperwork.
- **Exhibition labels & loan history** — verso labels, museum loans.
- **Literature references** — catalogue raisonné inclusion, books, articles.
- **Conservation / restoration records.**
- **Notable previous owners** — distinguished provenance commands a premium.

## 4. Market & economic factors (extrinsic)

- **Comparable sales** — recent, like-for-like transactions are the single strongest anchor.
- **Current demand / fashion** — for the tradition, style, and specific artist _right now_.
- **Auction indices & trend direction** — segment rising or cooling.
- **Geography of the market** — India domestic vs NRI/international demand.
- **Primary vs secondary market** — gallery list price vs resale.
- **Liquidity** — how readily this segment actually sells.
- **Macro** — currency (INR/USD), interest rates, wealth cycles, festive/wedding demand.
- **Sales channel & seasonality** — auction vs gallery vs online vs direct.
- **Transaction costs** — buyer's premium, commissions, taxes (affect net realisable value).

## 5. Legal, authenticity & risk

- **Authenticity / forgery risk** — and scientific examination where warranted.
- **Cultural-property & antiquities law** — GI tags (e.g. Mithila Painting GI), and for
  works/objects over ~100 years the Antiquities and Art Treasures Act, export permits.
- **Copyright & reproduction rights** — especially for living artists.
- **Clear legal title** — disputes, liens, restitution claims.
- **Insurance considerations.**

## 6. Valuation purpose — _which_ value?

The same work has different correct values depending on why you're valuing it. The app
should let the user pick:

- **Fair Market Value** — willing buyer/seller, no compulsion (the default).
- **Insurance / replacement value** — retail cost to replace; usually the **highest**.
- **Auction estimate** — expected hammer range (low/high).
- **Marketable cash / liquidation value** — forced/quick sale; usually the **lowest**.
- **Wholesale vs retail**, and donation/tax appraisal value.

## 7. Image & data-quality inputs (for AI accuracy)

- Photo resolution, lighting, colour accuracy, full-frame vs crop.
- **Multiple views** — front, verso/back, signature close-up, detail, a scale reference.
- Verso information — labels, stamps, inscriptions.
